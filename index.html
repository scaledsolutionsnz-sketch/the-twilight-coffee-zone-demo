The early stop on Miller Street

The Twilight Coffee Zone is a locally owned takeaway in the middle of Dannevirke. The coffee machine is running before sunrise for farmers, tradies and anyone heading down State Highway 2 with a long drive ahead.

Through the week it is coffee, pies, toasties and donuts. From Wednesday to Friday the grill fires back up at 5pm for burger nights, and the spicy chicken combo has built a following of its own.

  • Locally owned and run in Dannevirke
  • Open from 6am on weekdays
  • Everything cooked fresh to order
